# Disco Semantics

Disco Semantics is an ongoing process to
understand perception and its effect on meaning.
Field recordings of these perceptions are put
to type and pictograph by numerous
investigators.

It is the intention of this series to persist as
long as there are characters to fill it.
In future, if you feel so inclined, you can
attempt a perception, and you may wish to record
such attempts.

-----------------------------------------------

If you would like to contribute such records to
enhance a future issue,
Disco Semantics welcomes
you.
